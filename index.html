Head to List, With Love

A voice-first workflow that captures everything you are already thinking while you are driving between jobs. You talk. The system listens. You get a clean working document by the time you pull into the next driveway.

No typing while driving. No app to learn. No system to maintain. Just talk.

05

How It Works For You

Between Jobs — Record

Leaving the Hendersons. Talk through what you did, what you need, what you promised, what broke. Thirty seconds. Done.

End of Day — Process

Everything you said all day gets pulled into tasks, inventory notes, client updates, and follow-ups. Automatically. Dated. Bucketed.

Start of Day — Review

One clean list. What is on the truck. Who needs a call. What to pick up at the supply house. What your guy needs to know.

06

What Gets Captured

Client Layer

Equipment at each address. What was done. What was quoted. What they said. What you promised.

Truck Layer

What you used. What is low. What needs to be on tomorrow's list before you leave the supply house.

Job Layer

Sequencing. Access notes. Who to call before you show up. Commercial contact names. Building manager preferences.

Business Layer

Callbacks. Quotes to follow up. Warranty items. Seasonal prep. What your guy needs before he goes out.
